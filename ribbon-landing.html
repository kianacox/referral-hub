The actual questions you'll have.

§ vii · FAQ

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

Yes — completely free. Ribbon earns a small commission from partner brands (Amazon, Deliveroo, etc.) when you redeem points, similar to credit-card cashback. You never pay a fee. Your landlord doesn't pay a fee. There's no premium tier you get nudged toward. I've been using it 14 months and haven't paid a penny.

Does my landlord need to sign up or approve this?

No. Your landlord receives rent over normal Faster Payments, from your name, with your usual reference. They see exactly what they saw before — a bank transfer from you on rent day. There's nothing to tell them.

What happens to my money if Ribbon goes bust?

Your money isn't held by Ribbon — it sits in your named account at Griffin Bank, a fully licensed UK bank regulated by the FCA and PRA. Eligible deposits are protected by the FSCS up to £85,000, exactly like your high-street bank. Even if Ribbon stopped operating tomorrow, your deposits would remain protected at Griffin.

How long does it take to set up?

Sign-up took me about 4 minutes — name, address, employment, rent details. ID verification is fully digital. The slowest part is updating your standing order with your bank, which takes another few minutes online. You only ever do it once.

What can I actually redeem the points on?

Gift cards at over 40 brands: Amazon, Apple, Deliveroo, Nike, Nando's, Hotels.com, Eurostar, Ryanair, ASOS, Boots, M&S, Netflix, Spotify, John Lewis, Asda — and Avios points if you collect those. Points convert at a fixed rate (100 points = £1) and Ribbon adds new brands fairly regularly.

Will this affect my credit score?

No. Ribbon performs an identity check during sign-up (KYC), but it's a "soft" check — it doesn't appear on your credit report or affect your score. There's no credit being extended, so no hard search.
