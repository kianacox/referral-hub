Eligibility

Who qualifies for the £50?

The bonus is for people brand new to Lloyds. Quick check below — see if you're eligible before you start your application.

You're eligible if…

  • You're a UK resident aged 18 or over
  • You're completely new to Lloyds — no existing current account, savings, credit card, loan or mortgage
  • You're opening the account in your sole name
  • You've never received a Lloyds refer-a-friend bonus before
  • You can keep the new account open for at least 7 days

Sorry, not this time if…

  • You're applying for a joint account
  • You're an existing Lloyds, Halifax or Bank of Scotland customer
  • You've previously claimed a Lloyds refer-a-friend payment
  • You're opening an Under 19s, Smart Start or Student account
  • You're under 18 or not resident in the UK

The fine print

Terms & conditions

Clear, summarised — full T&Cs are on Lloyds' own application page when you click through.

  • Referral links are valid for 30 days from when they're generated. After that, a fresh link is required.
  • You must keep the new account open for a minimum of 7 days from the date it's opened.
  • The account must be in your sole name. Joint accounts don't qualify for the £50.
  • You must be new to Lloyds — no current account, savings, credit card, loan or mortgage held in the last 12 months.
  • The referrer's £50 is paid into their oldest qualifying Lloyds current account.
  • Under 19s, Smart Start and Student accounts are excluded from the refer-a-friend programme.
  • Each customer can only claim once as a referee under the Lloyds refer-a-friend programme.
  • Lloyds may change or withdraw the refer-a-friend offer at any time. The full T&Cs on the Lloyds website apply.
